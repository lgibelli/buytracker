🛒 BuyTracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

BuyTracker has no accounts, analytics or ads. It sends nothing to the developer unless you choose to, as described under Reports you choose to send.

What the extension stores

Your list of watched pages, their price history, and your settings are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage. They never leave your device except as described below.

Optional AI offer detection

This feature is off by default. If you enable it and provide your own Anthropic (Claude) API key, the text of a page you have chosen to watch is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to detect prices and offers. Your API key is stored locally in your browser and is used only to authenticate these requests. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Optional self-hosted parsing helper

Also off by default. BuyTracker can optionally use a small "recipe" service that you deploy yourself on your own Cloudflare account (see the project README). If you configure its URL and token in settings, a reduced snapshot of a page you have chosen to watch — with emails/phone numbers redacted and personal-looking fields stripped — is sent from your browser to your own service, which asks Anthropic once per site how to parse it. The developer never receives any of this data.

Reports you choose to send

Nothing below is sent unless you act. Neither kind of report carries an account, an install ID or any other identifier, so reports can't be linked to each other or to you.

  • Wrong-result reports. When a watch shows the wrong price or stock, you can report it from the popup. The report goes to the developer only when you tick “Also send this report to the developer”, or press “Send to the developer” (shown when you don't run your own recipe service). It contains the page address without its query string or fragment (nothing after “?” or “#”), the price, currency and stock state BuyTracker read, how it read the page (the parsing rules in use, if any, and the last error), your correction and optional note, the extension version, and, if your own recipe service re-checked the page, what it read then.
  • Error reports. Off by default: turn on “Send error reports to the developer” in Settings. When BuyTracker hits a bug in its own code, it sends the error type and message, where in BuyTracker's code it happened, the extension version, and your Chrome version and operating system (from the browser's user agent). Web addresses, email addresses and quoted text are removed first, and nothing about your watches is included. Each distinct error is sent at most once a day.

Reports go to a service the developer runs on Cloudflare (buytracker-recipes.nervoso.workers.dev) and are stored in a Cloudflare database: wrong-result reports for up to a year, error reports for 90 days. They are used only to fix how BuyTracker reads stores and to fix bugs, and are never sold or shared.

To limit abuse, the service counts reports per day by a keyed hash of your IP address. The hash can't be turned back into the address, is never stored with a report, and is deleted the next day. Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, handles the connection under its own privacy policy.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or accounts.
  • No identifiers: the reports you choose to send are anonymous.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
