🛒 BuyTracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

BuyTracker has no accounts, analytics or ads. It sends nothing to the developer unless you choose to, as described under Recipe service and Reports you choose to send.

What the extension stores

Your list of watched pages, their price history, and your settings are stored locally in your browser's own extension storage. They never leave your device except as described below.

Optional AI offer detection

This feature is off by default. If you enable it and provide your own Anthropic (Claude) API key, the text of a page you have chosen to watch is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to detect prices and offers. Your API key is stored locally in your browser and is used only to authenticate these requests. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Recipe service, store by store

When BuyTracker can't read a store on its own, that store's watch offers the free recipe service, which the developer runs on Cloudflare (buytracker-recipes.nervoso.workers.dev). Nothing about a store is sent until you allow that store, on its watch or by submitting a “Report & fix” for it. You can remove a store, or turn the service off, in Settings.

For a store you allowed, BuyTracker sends the product page's address without its query string or fragment, and a short extract of the page: its title, the prices and buttons near the product with their positions in the page, the start of its text, and data the page publishes for its own scripts. Email addresses and phone numbers are redacted first, and data named like account, customer, cart or session details is left out. An AI model on Cloudflare (Workers AI) turns the extract into a “recipe”, the rules for where this store shows price and stock. The service does not keep the extract. Once two different connections get the same recipe for a store on the same day, the service keeps it and gives it to other users of that store; until then it holds the day's keyed hash of each connection's IP address with that recipe, and deletes it at the end of the day. BuyTracker sends again only when the recipe stops reading the page (at most once a week) or when you report a wrong result. All checks run in your browser.

The service is shared and free, so it has a daily limit, and it counts requests per day by a keyed hash of your IP address, as described for reports below.

If you run your own copy of the service (Settings, “Your own recipe worker”), BuyTracker uses it for every store instead, and the developer receives none of this.

Reports you choose to send

Nothing below is sent unless you act. Neither kind of report carries an account, an install ID or any other identifier, so reports can't be linked to each other or to you.

  • Wrong-result reports. When a watch shows the wrong price or stock, you can report it from the popup. The report goes to the developer only when you tick “Also send this report to the developer”, or press “Send to the developer” (shown when the recipe service is off). It contains the page address without its query string or fragment (nothing after “?” or “#”), the price, currency and stock state BuyTracker read, how it read the page (the parsing rules in use, if any, and the last error), your correction and optional note, the extension version, and, if the recipe service re-checked the page, what it read then.
  • Error reports. Off by default: turn on “Send error reports to the developer” in Settings. When BuyTracker hits a bug in its own code, it sends the error type and message, where in BuyTracker's code it happened, the extension version, and your browser (Chrome or Firefox) version and operating system (from the browser's user agent). Web addresses, email addresses and quoted text are removed first, and nothing about your watches is included. Each distinct error is sent at most once a day.

Reports go to a service the developer runs on Cloudflare (buytracker-recipes.nervoso.workers.dev) and are stored in a Cloudflare database: wrong-result reports for up to a year, error reports for 90 days. They are used only to fix how BuyTracker reads stores and to fix bugs, and are never sold or shared.

To limit abuse, the service counts requests per day by a keyed hash of your IP address. The hash can't be turned back into the address, is never stored with a report, and is deleted the next day. Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, handles the connection under its own privacy policy.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or accounts.
  • No identifiers: what you choose to send is anonymous.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
