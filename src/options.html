Most stores are read automatically, for free. For the few BuyTracker can't parse on its own, the free recipe service can learn them, and you decide which stores it hears about.

Recipe service Free

For the few stores the built-in reader can't parse. The recipe service works out how to read a store once; after that, every check runs in your browser, with no AI and nothing sent.

Nothing about a store is sent until you allow that store on its watch. Then BuyTracker sends the product page's address (without anything after “?”) and a short extract of it (title, prices, buttons, opening text): once, and again only if the recipe stops reading the page. The service is shared and free, so it has a daily limit; past it, new stores wait until the next day. Privacy policy

    Your own recipe worker Advanced self-hosted alternative

    Run your own copy of the recipe service on Cloudflare's free plan. BuyTracker then uses it for every store, without asking per store, and nothing goes to the shared service. Setup guide

    Your worker's AUTH_TOKEN secret: it lets your corrections be logged on your worker. Sent only to your worker URL, nowhere else.

    Defaults

    Checks run only while your browser is open (extension-only mode).

    Error reports

    Off unless you turn it on. When BuyTracker hits a bug in its own code, it sends the error message, where in the code it happened, your extension and browser versions. Web addresses and page text are removed first, and nothing about your watches is included. No account, no ID. Each error is sent at most once a day. Privacy policy

    Direct Claude API Experimental pay-per-check alternative

    Instead of recipes, paste an Anthropic API key and BuyTracker calls Claude directly whenever a page has no structured price. Simplest to switch on, but you pay per check rather than once per site. Without either option, unreadable pages just fall back to a plain "this page changed" nudge.

    Stored locally in this browser only; sent only to Anthropic. Get a key →

    Cost: billed by Anthropic directly, only for checks that use the fallback, structured-price sites stay free. Each call sends ~4k tokens of page text for a tiny reply, so on Haiku it runs well under a cent (~$0.003). A site that never exposes a structured price, checked every 6h, is ~4 calls/day (roughly 1–2¢/day, under $1/month). A recipe removes even that.
