You're all set

BuyTracker watches product pages for price drops, new offers, and restocks. Here are the three ways to start a watch.

Watch a whole page

Open a product page, click the BuyTracker icon, and hit Watch this page. It tracks the price and stock and builds a price history.

Watch just an area

Click + Area and select one region, a promo banner, a coupon, a specific variant's price, to watch only that part of the page.

Right-click anything

Right-click a product page or a link and choose Monitor price changes or Monitor availability (restock) from the BuyTracker menu.

Tricky store? Optional

Most stores are read automatically, for free. For the few BuyTracker can't parse on its own, set up the recipe worker so it learns them once.

Checks run while your browser is open.
